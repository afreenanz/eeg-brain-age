### Findings requiring action
1. **Sex imbalance (AD 67% female vs HC 38%).** This is a potential confound for every AD-vs-HC
   comparison. Action: task B1 adds sex (and age) as covariates.
2. **AD subjects retain fewer clean epochs** (median 324 vs 367). Spectral estimates are noisier
   for some AD subjects. Action: test whether BAG depends on epoch count (B1) and on the
   rejection threshold (B4). The CNN uses ≤150 epochs per subject, which largely equalizes input
   size across groups.
3. Amplitude scale and recording duration do not differ between groups, so units and scaling
   are consistent.

- The highest between-subject feature correlation is r = 0.96 (ds004504 sub-024 vs sub-028, both

  the same way.

## Cross-dataset differences (by design, documented)
- **Equipment and protocol:** ds004504 is a clinical Nihon Kohden system with a 19-channel cap
  and an A1-A2 recording reference. Dortmund and LEMON are research BrainVision/actiCAP systems
  with an FCz reference and 62-64 channels. All data are reduced to the same 19 channels,
  average-referenced over those 19, filtered 0.5-45 Hz and resampled to 250 Hz.
- **Recording length:** ds004504 is ~13 min continuous, Dortmund 3 min, and LEMON 8 × 1 min
  eyes-closed blocks.
- **Age coverage:** the healthy training set covers 20-70 y (LEMON adds 55-80, in 5-year bins).
  The ds004504 HC are 57-78 y, so some prediction is extrapolation beyond 70 (examined in B2).
